Privacy Policy for MACT Analytics

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Taruna Deora built the MACT Analytics app as an ad-supported application with an optional one-time in-app purchase. This service is provided by Taruna Deora and is intended for use as is.

Information Collection and Use

MACT Analytics stores your app preferences and Pro upgrade entitlement locally on your device. However, the app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you (such as device identifiers, IP address, crash diagnostics, and usage interactions):

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing (for app distribution and one-time in-app purchases)
  • Firebase Authentication (for user authentication)
  • Google AdMob (for displaying advertisements in the free version)

You can review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local app data can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the application. If you signed in via Firebase Authentication and wish to request account or data deletion, please contact us at the email address below.

Children's Privacy

This service does not address anyone under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
