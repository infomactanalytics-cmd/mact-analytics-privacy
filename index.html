Privacy Policy & Account Deletion — MACT Analytics

Effective Date: November 2026
Developer: MACT Analytics Legal Tech (info.mactanalytics@gmail.com)

1. Overview

MACT Analytics is an independent legal calculation and research utility designed for advocates and claimants. All compensation calculations (Fatal, Injury, Interest, and RSLSA schedules) are computed locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your case calculation inputs to external servers.

2. Information Collected & Third-Party Services

To provide authentication, monetization, and app functionality, MACT Analytics uses the following Google services:

  • Firebase Authentication: If you choose to create an account, your email address and display name are processed securely by Google Firebase solely for login authentication. Guest users do not provide personal credentials.
  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchases (mact_pro_upgrade) are processed directly by Google Play. We do not access or store your card or bank details; we only receive a purchase verification token to unlock Pro features.
  • Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP): Free-tier users see banner advertisements served by Google AdMob, which may collect anonymous Device/Advertising IDs and IP-based location signals for ad delivery and fraud prevention in accordance with your consent choices.

3. Account & Data Deletion Policy

Users of MACT Analytics can permanently delete their account and associated authentication credentials at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Open MACT Analytics → Open the side menu → Tap My Profile → Scroll to Danger Zone → Tap Delete Account and confirm. Your Firebase Authentication record (email address and display name) is erased immediately.
  • Web/Email Deletion Request: If you no longer have the app installed, email info.mactanalytics@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Account Deletion Request", and your account and associated authentication data will be permanently purged within 48 hours.

4. Children's Privacy

MACT Analytics is intended for adult legal professionals and claimants and does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Legal Disclaimer

MACT Analytics is an independent private software tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representative of any Motor Accidents Claims Tribunal (MACT), High Court, Supreme Court, RSLSA, or government entity.
